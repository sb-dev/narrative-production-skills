# Film Handoff

**Level 5 — Produce narrative artifacts for downstream film production**

An overnight cleaner discovers that office meeting rooms are replaying conversations scheduled for the next morning.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a five-minute near-future drama and prepare a clean narrative handoff for downstream video production.

Premise:
An overnight cleaner discovers that office meeting rooms are replaying conversations scheduled for the next morning. She eventually hears a betrayal she was never meant to know about and must decide whether to intervene.

Requirements:
- Length: approximately five minutes of screen story
- Genre: near-future drama with a speculative premise
- Tone: quiet, restrained, morally tense
- Protagonist: Imani, a night cleaner who is observant and professionally invisible to most office staff
- Imani wants to keep her job and avoid entanglement
- Her deeper conflict is whether invisibility remains morally defensible once foreknowledge gives her agency
- Do not turn her into a conventional action hero
- The final decision should be small but consequential

Workflow:
- Develop the story brief, character profile and only the world rules required by the premise
- Build a compact screenplay or scene-level story plan
- Preserve Imani's behavioural constraints throughout development
- Prepare explicit handoff artifacts for downstream production
- Include a story brief, character profile, world/story constraints, screenplay or script material, and scene plans where useful
- Keep visual character design, storyboards, cinematography, shot design and video generation out of the Narrative Production Skills scope
- Make downstream creative freedom clear wherever the narrative does not require a specific visual choice

Final-scene requirement:
Imani has confirmed that the rooms replay future conversations. She enters the final meeting room to determine whether the betrayal she overheard will actually harm someone. Intervening risks exposing how she learned about it and may cost her job. She decides silence would make her complicit, sends one anonymous message, and leaves before the morning staff arrive.

What to optimise for:
- clear dramatic causality
- restrained protagonist behaviour
- a morally meaningful final choice
- minimal but sufficient world rules
- handoff artifacts that preserve story intent without prescribing visual production
```

## What this example demonstrates

```text
Narrative Production Skills
→ story brief / character profile / world constraints / screenplay / scene plan
→ Video Production Skills
→ optional Music Production Skills
```

The handoff is artifact-based. Narrative artifacts preserve story intent and constraints; downstream projects own their domain execution.

| Path | What it is |

| `handoff/character-profile.md` | Imani's narrative identity, motivation and behavioural constraints |
