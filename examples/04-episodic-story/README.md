# Episodic Story

**Level 4 — Maintain story movement and character state across episodes**

Each episode follows a different emergency call routed through the same impossible switchboard, while one operator slowly realises the calls come from future disasters.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop an episodic speculative drama built around an emergency-call operator receiving calls from future disasters.

Series premise:
A municipal emergency operator receives one impossible call per episode. Each call initially appears self-contained, but details accumulate into evidence of a future city-wide catastrophe.

Requirements:
- Each episode must have a satisfying local emergency story
- The serial arc must advance without overwhelming the episode story
- The operator's knowledge should evolve gradually
- Separate what the operator knows from what the audience can infer
- Future catastrophe material may remain planned until established on the page
- Avoid repeating the same reveal structure every episode
- Preserve previously established facts and character decisions

Episode 1:
A caller reports being trapped in a station tunnel that is currently dry and open. The operator initially treats the call as a prank until the caller predicts an electrical failure that occurs minutes later.

Workflow:
- Define the series concept and recurring dramatic engine
- Establish the operator's initial character state and longer arc
- Outline Episode 1 as a complete story with its own turn and consequence
- Record which information is canonical, which is character knowledge, and which remains planned future material
- Draft only the episode material needed for this level
- Evaluate both episode coherence and serial progression
- Revise locally while preserving established series decisions

What to optimise for:
- episode-level causality
- cumulative mystery
- evolving character knowledge
- serial setup and payoff
- continuity across episodes
- distinction between planned future events and established canon
```

## What this example demonstrates

This level adds persistent story state because a single flat outline no longer carries enough context safely.

```text
series concept
→ episode story
→ evolving character state
→ continuity
→ progressive evaluation
```
