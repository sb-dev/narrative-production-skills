# Local Legends Club

**Level 4 — Manage an episodic story**

**Genre:** children's adventure / mystery

Four children investigate one neighbourhood legend per episode and gradually discover that the supposedly unrelated stories describe the same hidden route through their town.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a three-episode children's adventure series package titled “Local Legends Club”.

Series premise:
Four children start a club to investigate local legends around their town: a bell that rings under the river, chalk arrows that reappear after rain, and a fox said to wait outside houses before somebody moves away. Each episode tackles one legend as a complete adventure. Across the episodes, details begin to suggest the legends all describe parts of the same forgotten route.

Requirements:
- Genre: children's adventure / mystery
- Audience: upper-middle-grade / family
- Tone: curious, funny, adventurous, never grim
- Each episode must work as a complete local investigation
- The children should have complementary strengths and genuine disagreements
- Adults may be mistaken or incomplete but should not all be useless
- Keep danger age-appropriate
- Track clues, beliefs and discoveries carefully across episodes
- The serial route mystery should advance without making each episode feel incomplete

Deliverables:
- concise series concept and rules
- four core character profiles
- three episode outlines with distinct locations and legend types
- clue/continuity record distinguishing observed fact, child theory and planned reveal
- detailed scene plan for one discovery scene

Workflow:
- Define a repeatable investigation engine suitable for the audience
- Design three different legends before connecting them serially
- Track which child knows or believes each clue
- Evaluate episode completeness, age fit, clue fairness and team progression
- Revise locally if a clue arrives too early or a character acts on knowledge they do not have

What to optimise for:
- adventurous momentum
- child agency
- varied episode premises
- clue continuity
- evolving friendship dynamics
- a serial mystery that rewards attention without overwhelming local stories
```

## What this example demonstrates

Prove repeatable episode engines, persistent ensembles, planned-versus-canonical separation and growing continuity across contrasting series genres.

```text
series engine → episode stories → evolving character state → continuity → progressive evaluation
```

This example is one of three productions at Level 4. The level is the capability target; the different stories test whether that capability transfers across genres, tones and audience expectations.
