# Second Chance Café

**Level 4 — Manage an episodic story**

**Genre:** romantic ensemble series

Each episode follows a customer using a neighbourhood café to attempt one second chance, while the staff's own relationships change across the season.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a three-episode romantic ensemble package titled “Second Chance Café”.

Series premise:
A neighbourhood café quietly becomes known as the place people choose when they want one conversation they should have had years ago. Each episode centres on a different customer's attempted second chance, while owner Mina, chef Jay and barista Leila develop their own evolving relationships and unresolved histories.

Requirements:
- Genre: romantic / relationship ensemble
- Tone: warm, observant, emotionally specific, not sentimental
- Each episode must have a self-contained customer story with a distinct relationship problem
- A second chance does not have to mean reconciliation or romance
- Staff arcs should progress in small cumulative steps across episodes
- Avoid repeating the same emotional structure three times
- Track what recurring characters know about one another and which histories remain private

Deliverables:
- series premise and repeatable episode engine
- profiles and three-episode arc for the recurring staff
- outlines for three materially different customer stories
- continuity state for staff relationships, disclosures and unresolved possibilities
- detailed scene plan for one emotionally pivotal café conversation

Workflow:
- Define the episode engine in terms of dramatic function, not plot formula
- Design three customer stories with different outcomes and tones
- Map recurring staff progression independently
- Track disclosures as they move from private to shared knowledge
- Evaluate episode variety, emotional credibility and serial progression
- Revise the smallest affected episode or relationship beat

What to optimise for:
- relationship specificity
- varied emotional outcomes
- sustainable ensemble engine
- continuity of recurring relationships
- subtext
- satisfying local endings without forced closure
```

## What this example demonstrates

Prove repeatable episode engines, persistent ensembles, planned-versus-canonical separation and growing continuity across contrasting series genres.

```text
series engine → episode stories → evolving character state → continuity → progressive evaluation
```

This example is one of three productions at Level 4. The level is the capability target; the different stories test whether that capability transfers across genres, tones and audience expectations.
