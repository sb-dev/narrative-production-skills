# The Orchestra in the Walls

**Level 5 — Prepare a narrative handoff for audiovisual production**

**Genre:** magical realism / music-driven drama

A lonely tenant hears one isolated instrument through each wall of her flat and gradually realises the separate fragments form one unfinished composition.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a 6–8 minute short-film narrative titled “The Orchestra in the Walls” and prepare a clean Narrative → Music → Video production handoff.

Premise:
New tenant Ana hears a cello phrase through one wall of her flat, a clarinet response through another, and percussion from the ceiling. The neighbours insist they are not musicians. Over several nights the isolated fragments begin fitting together into one unfinished composition whose missing final phrase is connected to the flat's previous tenant.

Requirements:
- Genre: magical realism / music-driven drama
- Tone: intimate, curious, emotionally restrained
- Keep the supernatural explanation optional; the story must work through Ana's choices
- Define what the music means narratively without composing the actual score
- Build scenes around listening, investigation and human connection
- Avoid prescribing camera language, lighting, production design, exact instrumentation arrangement or final musical composition
- End with a human action that gives the unfinished music new meaning

Narrative deliverables:
- story brief
- protagonist and supporting-character profiles
- minimal story rules
- scene-level screenplay or script
- scene plans identifying narrative music functions where relevant
- handoff notes separating required story information from downstream Music and Video creative freedom

Workflow:
- Develop and evaluate the story before downstream production
- Specify only the musical facts required for causality and payoff
- Identify which decisions belong to Music Production Skills
- Identify which decisions belong to Video Production Skills
- Preserve approved narrative constraints through handoff
- Do not create score composition, shot plans or visual design inside Narrative Production Skills

What to optimise for:
- story/music interdependence without over-specifying music
- emotional progression
- clear cross-domain boundaries
- useful downstream artifacts
- visual and musical production freedom
- a final payoff that works narratively before production choices are made
```

## What this example demonstrates

Prove artifact-based composition with downstream Video, Animation and Music Production Skills while preserving the boundary between story decisions and production decisions.

```text
narrative package → story constraints / screenplay / scene plans → downstream audiovisual and music production
```

This example is one of three productions at Level 5. The level is the capability target; the different stories test whether that capability transfers across genres, tones and audience expectations.
