# Screenplay

**Level 3 — Develop a screenplay scene from narrative artifacts**

A housing officer must interview the tenant of a flat that city records insist has never existed.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop and write a screenplay scene for a restrained supernatural drama.

Premise:
A housing officer is sent to verify the occupancy of a flat that the city's records insist has never existed.

Scene requirements:
- The scene takes place at night inside flat 1408 of a council tower
- Nadia, the housing officer, enters believing the problem is an administrative error
- Mr Vale, the tenant, should remain calm and matter-of-fact rather than overtly threatening
- Nadia's immediate objective is to verify occupancy and close the case
- Mr Vale knows details from Nadia's childhood that are not public
- The turn is a tenancy agreement signed in Nadia's handwriting twenty years earlier
- Nadia has never knowingly visited the building
- End with Nadia unable to treat the flat as a clerical anomaly

Workflow:
- Create a scene card before writing screenplay pages
- Make the scene objective, conflict, turn and exit state explicit
- Preserve the continuity requirement that Nadia has never knowingly visited the building
- Draft the scene in Fountain-compatible screenplay format
- Evaluate dramatic function, character behaviour, dialogue, continuity and scene turn without silently rewriting
- Create a bounded revision plan for any material defect
- Revise only the affected scene material

What to optimise for:
- concise visual action that remains narrative rather than cinematographic direction
- subtext rather than exposition
- clear objective and conflict
- a strong scene turn
- restrained dialogue
- continuity with the established premise
```

## What this example demonstrates

```text
story intent
→ scene card
→ screenplay scene
→ developmental evaluation
→ targeted revision
```

Narrative Production Skills owns the dramatic scene and screenplay text. Storyboarding, shot design, cinematography and visual production remain downstream responsibilities.
