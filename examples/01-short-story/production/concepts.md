# Concept Draft Set

## A — Warning Train
The impossible service appears minutes before fatal network failures. The dispatcher must decide whether to break protocol based on a signal no one else can see.

## B — Lost Commuters
The train carries names of people officially missing from the network over decades; each appearance gives the dispatcher one chance to recover a new clue.

## C — Duplicate Network
The train belongs to a second version of the rail network whose routing decisions are beginning to overwrite the real one.

**Selected:** A. It creates the strongest contained causal engine and a decision-focused ending.
