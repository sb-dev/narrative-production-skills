# Rough Scene — First Appearance

At 02:17, Mara saw a train enter the old Wandsworth branch.

The branch had been disconnected for eleven years. She knew this because the diagram still showed the dead section in grey, a thin scar at the edge of the live network.

Yet a white block moved through it now: **7X91**.

Mara leaned closer. No route call. No driver ID. No consist. Just the number and a progress bar advancing towards a junction that no longer had points.

She reached for the internal phone, then stopped when the block vanished.

The grey branch was empty again.
