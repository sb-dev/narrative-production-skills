# Selected Outline

1. Mara settles into the quietest hour of the night shift and notices service 7X91 crossing a closed junction.
2. No timetable, driver, depot or signalling record contains 7X91.
3. The service appears again immediately before a points failure nearly causes a collision.
4. Mara discovers archive incident logs containing the same ghost identifier before three historic disasters.
5. When 7X91 appears on a converging line, Mara breaks protocol and reroutes a passenger service.
6. The predicted failure occurs on the original route; the rerouted train survives.
7. 7X91 disappears from the board. Mara logs the impossible event rather than erasing it.
