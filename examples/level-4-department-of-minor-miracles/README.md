# Department of Minor Miracles

**Level 4 — Manage an episodic story**

**Genre:** workplace fantasy comedy

A municipal office investigates small supernatural inconveniences that are too trivial for emergency services but too impossible for ordinary departments.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a three-episode workplace-fantasy comedy package titled “Department of Minor Miracles”.

Series premise:
In an underfunded municipal office, civil servants investigate supernatural incidents officially classified as “minor”: a vending machine that gives prophetic snacks, a goldfish that cannot die, and umbrellas that levitate only inside one postcode. Each case is locally ridiculous but leaves evidence that the frequency of minor miracles is increasing.

Requirements:
- Genre: workplace fantasy comedy
- Build a repeatable case-of-the-week engine
- Each episode must resolve its own minor miracle problem
- Maintain a serial thread about why miracle frequency is increasing
- Keep the rules for recurring supernatural phenomena consistent
- Develop an ensemble whose workplace relationships evolve independently of the cases
- Comedy should come from institutional process colliding with impossible facts, not from characters being incompetent

Deliverables:
- concise series concept and operating rules
- core ensemble profiles
- three distinct episode outlines
- serial progression across Episodes 1–3
- detailed scene plan for one pivotal scene
- continuity state for recurring rules, relationships, beliefs and planned revelations

Workflow:
- Define the episode engine and what qualifies as a “minor miracle”
- Design three cases with materially different comic problems
- Track workplace relationships and serial clues separately from case plots
- Update continuity after each episode outline
- Evaluate repeatability, episode variety, comic engine and serial movement
- Revise locally without rebuilding stable series rules

What to optimise for:
- sustainable episode engine
- ensemble chemistry
- institutional satire without cynicism
- varied cases
- cumulative serial movement
- continuity across recurring rules and relationships
```

## What this example demonstrates

Prove repeatable episode engines, persistent ensembles, planned-versus-canonical separation and growing continuity across contrasting series genres.

```text
series engine → episode stories → evolving character state → continuity → progressive evaluation
```

This example is one of three productions at Level 4. The level is the capability target; the different stories test whether that capability transfers across genres, tones and audience expectations.
