# The Day Gravity Blinked

**Level 5 — Prepare a narrative handoff for audiovisual production**

**Genre:** science-fiction comedy

Gravity disappears for exactly three seconds every hour while a baker tries to deliver an elaborate wedding cake across town before the next scheduled blink.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a 6–8 minute live-action short-film narrative titled “The Day Gravity Blinked” and prepare a clean handoff for downstream video production.

Premise:
For reasons nobody understands, gravity disappears everywhere for exactly three seconds at the top of each hour. By lunchtime the city has adapted badly. Baker Jo Alvarez must transport a fragile multi-tier wedding cake across town, with twelve minutes left before the next gravity blink and a client who has already survived three wedding disasters that morning.

Requirements:
- Genre: science-fiction comedy
- Tone: energetic, inventive, humane
- Establish the three-second gravity rule early and never change it for convenience
- Escalation should come from Jo's route choices, preparation and the consequences of earlier blinks
- Keep the story contained enough for a short film
- The cake matters because of Jo's professional and personal stakes, not because the universe depends on it
- Narrative may specify story-required physical consequences but must not prescribe VFX technique, stunt design, camera moves, lenses, lighting or edit grammar

Narrative deliverables:
- brief and protagonist profile
- gravity rule / world constraints
- causal beat sheet
- screenplay or detailed scene plan
- handoff notes identifying must-preserve physical story facts versus downstream production choices

Workflow:
- Define and approve the gravity rule before plotting
- Build escalation from repeated use of that one rule
- Evaluate causality, comic rhythm and rule consistency before handoff
- Identify downstream visual-production challenges without solving them inside Narrative
- Preserve story constraints while leaving Video Production Skills room to design VFX, shots and editing

What to optimise for:
- rule-driven comedy
- clean physical causality
- protagonist agency
- escalating set pieces at story level
- cross-domain boundary discipline
- production-ready narrative constraints without audiovisual micromanagement
```

## What this example demonstrates

Prove artifact-based composition with downstream Video, Animation and Music Production Skills while preserving the boundary between story decisions and production decisions.

```text
narrative package → story constraints / screenplay / scene plans → downstream audiovisual and music production
```

This example is one of three productions at Level 5. The level is the capability target; the different stories test whether that capability transfers across genres, tones and audience expectations.
