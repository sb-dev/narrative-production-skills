# Paper Moon Parade

**Level 5 — Prepare a narrative handoff for audiovisual production**

**Genre:** family fantasy / animated short

A child's folded paper animals come alive and lead her across the city to find the elderly neighbour who taught her how to make them.

## Prompt

```text
Use Narrative Production Skills to develop a 6–8 minute animated short-film narrative titled “Paper Moon Parade” and prepare a clean handoff to downstream visual and music production.

Premise:
After her elderly neighbour Mr Ito unexpectedly moves away, nine-year-old Mara folds the last sheet of patterned paper he gave her into a fox. At dusk the fox unfolds itself, joins a procession of other paper animals emerging from places around the neighbourhood, and leads Mara toward something Mr Ito left behind.

Requirements:
- Medium target: animated short film
- Genre: family fantasy / adventure
- Audience: children / family
- Tone: wondrous, warm, gently funny
- Mara must drive the story through choices rather than simply follow magical creatures
- Establish only the narrative rules required for the paper animals
- The ending should resolve Mara's emotional need without undoing Mr Ito's departure
- Narrative may specify story-relevant animal identities or actions but must not define final visual style, animation technique, character-sheet design, cinematography or score

Narrative deliverables:
- story brief
- Mara character profile
- minimal fantasy rules
- compact screenplay or scene plan
- story-relevant paper-animal roles
- downstream handoff notes for Video/animation and optional Music Production Skills

Workflow:
- Explore multiple story structures before selecting one
- Stabilise the emotional arc and fantasy rule before screenplay execution
- Evaluate scene purpose, child agency and setup/payoff
- Prepare handoff artifacts that constrain story meaning but leave visual interpretation open
- Keep storyboard, animation style and soundtrack composition downstream

What to optimise for:
- child agency
- visualisable story action without visual over-direction
- emotional restraint
- clear fantasy rules
- family audience fit
- strong handoff freedom for animation and music
```

## What this example demonstrates

Prove artifact-based composition with downstream Video, Animation and Music Production Skills while preserving the boundary between story decisions and production decisions.

```text
narrative package → story constraints / screenplay / scene plans → downstream audiovisual and music production
```

This example is one of three productions at Level 5. The level is the capability target; the different stories test whether that capability transfers across genres, tones and audience expectations.
