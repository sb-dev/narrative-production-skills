# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific in technical and creative criticism.
- Critique work and ideas rather than people.
- Give context for disagreements and propose concrete alternatives where possible.
- Respect different creative traditions, genres, cultures, and working methods.
- Do not use project spaces for harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or unwanted sexual attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for serious or repeated violations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security/contact channels when available.
