# Narrative Revise

Convert diagnosis or direct feedback into bounded narrative change.

## Sequence

1. Identify the actual problem.
2. Identify the highest relevant root cause.
3. Choose the smallest sufficient revision scope.
4. State what must be preserved.
5. State what may change.
6. Identify downstream material actually affected.
7. Revise.
8. Verify the correction and preservation constraints.

## Scope

Possible scopes include local passage, scene, beat, sequence/section, character arc, story outline and deliberate page-one rewrite.

## Approved decisions

Approved work remains a constraint unless explicitly reopened. If a fix requires changing an approved upstream decision, identify it, explain why it must reopen, select the replacement direction and revise only affected descendants.

## Root-cause targeting

- weak prose + sound scene → prose revision;
- weak scene design + sound beat → scene-card revision then affected scene;
- unmotivated beat → beat revision then affected scenes;
- weak causal section → affected outline region;
- premise failure → only then reopen high-level concept.

## Verification

Confirm target issue improved, preserve constraints survived, continuity remains coherent and no major regression was introduced.

Do not default to page-one rewrite or automatic dependency propagation.
