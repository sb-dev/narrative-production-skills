# Narrative Evaluate

Diagnose narrative quality and readiness without silently replacing the target artifact.

## Operating sequence

1. Identify target artifact and lifecycle state.
2. Select only criteria meaningful at that resolution.
3. Ground findings in actual evidence.
4. Prioritise root causes over surface symptoms.
5. Produce actionable findings rather than an unsolicited rewrite.

## Lifecycle

### Draft

Check basic coherence, hard constraints, catastrophic contradiction, candidate distinctiveness and whether the artifact is worth developing.

### Refine

Verify requested correction, preservation, continuity and regressions.

### Final

Run all applicable developmental checks. Prioritise structural/developmental issues before copy polish.

## Quality dimensions

Use as applicable: premise, causality, character, conflict, stakes, structure, pacing, scene purpose, world consistency, continuity, POV, voice, dialogue, theme, tone, setup/payoff and genre/audience fit.

## Findings

Each significant finding should identify category, severity, scope, location, evidence, diagnosis, affected material and recommendation.

## Boundaries

Do not rewrite when evaluation only was requested. Do not force a universal story structure. Do not turn mechanical lint into a literary quality score. Do not require one numeric narrative-quality score.
