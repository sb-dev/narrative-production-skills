# Artifact Contract — Narrative Write

This skill consumes relevant `story_concept`, `character_profile`, `world_bible`, `story_outline`, `beat_sheet`, `scene_card`, `continuity_record`, preceding narrative and style requirements. It produces or refines `narrative_draft`, `manuscript`, or `screenplay`.

A rough draft proves narrative behaviour before polish. Approved upstream decisions remain constraints. A written discovery may justify a deliberate upstream update, but manuscript text must not silently become a second source of truth.
