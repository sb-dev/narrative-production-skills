# Narrative Develop

Develop the story at the lowest useful narrative resolution before spending effort on higher-resolution writing.

## Operating sequence

1. Identify the smallest relevant target: brief, concept, character, world, outline, beat sheet, or scene card.
2. Determine workflow state: `draft`, `refine`, or `final`.
3. Determine decision status where useful: `candidate`, `selected`, `approved`, `rejected`, or `superseded`.
4. Load only relevant upstream constraints.
5. Explore alternatives only where meaningful uncertainty remains.
6. Preserve selected and approved decisions.
7. Evaluate at the current narrative resolution before escalating.

## Core rules

- Prefer low-resolution concept/outline/beat/scene-card work before full prose when it can answer the question.
- Candidate alternatives must differ in story logic, not merely wording.
- Character material should affect story behaviour; do not default to encyclopaedic biographies.
- World material should support or constrain the story; do not create lore for completeness.
- Treat beats as meaningful changes with causes and consequences, not simply small scenes.
- A scene card records why the scene exists: entry state, objective, conflict, turn, exit state, information, and continuity needs.
- Branch at the smallest creative unit containing the uncertainty.
- No structural framework is mandatory.

## Refinement

When refining, state or infer:

```text
preserve
change
```

Change only the target deficiency and update downstream planning only where actually affected.

## Discovery writing

If prose reveals a better upstream decision, surface the discovery and update the relevant planning artifact deliberately. Do not let written narrative silently diverge from the story model.

## Evaluation

- Draft: useful enough to continue; no catastrophic contradiction; respects hard constraints.
- Refine: requested change worked; approved decisions survived; no major regression.
- Final: planning artifact is coherent and ready for its downstream consumer.

## Boundaries

Do not create visual character designs, storyboards, cinematography, model-provider integrations, graph/vector infrastructure, or unrelated world encyclopaedias.

Read the local references when the task requires deeper guidance.
